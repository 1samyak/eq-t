A SARETY PRODUCT
01 — The problem
The research bottleneck

Analysts don't lack data.
They lack a defensible trail.

Hours disappear into finding, reconciling and citing evidence before an analyst can form a view. The risk is not only slow research—it is a conclusion that cannot be checked.

The work between finding information and defending a thesis remains fragmented across documents, spreadsheets and research tools.
01

Source material is scattered

Annual reports, quarterly results, exchange disclosures and management commentary live in different places and formats.

02

Reconciliation is manual

Analysts normalize periods, compare statements and verify line items before interpretation can begin.

03

Earnings-quality signals are easy to miss

Cash-flow divergence, working-capital movements and disclosure changes need deliberate cross-checking.

04

Evidence gets separated from the conclusion

Source references, calculations and assumptions can become detached from the final research narrative.

Problem framing from the EQ-T product brief; no time-savings claim is asserted.02 / 12
